Privacy Policy

Larki Se Dosti keeps your profile on your device. There is no password. In this version, people, chats, gifts, and coins are sample data stored locally so you can try the app.

We ask for your age, name, gender, country, and an optional photo. Photos stay pending review until they are approved. We do not sell personal information.

Terms of use

Be 18 or older. Be kind. Do not harass anyone, and do not upload anything you do not have the right to share. We may remove an account that breaks these rules.

The app is for adults 18 and older. If you are under 18, you cannot create an account.
