Child Safety Standards

Larki Se Dosti is strictly for adults 18 and older. Sign-up stops anyone who enters an age under 18.

We do not allow child sexual content, grooming, or any sexual or romantic content involving minors. Profiles, chat, and calls include Report and Block. Uploaded photos stay pending review until they are approved.

If you see a minor or other harmful content, report the user in the app and email support@larkisedosti.app. We will remove the content and may ban the account.
