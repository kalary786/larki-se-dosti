Child safety standards

Larki Se Dosti is an adults-only app. You must confirm you are 18 or older before an account is created. We do not allow children to use the service.

Our standards

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), is prohibited. That includes any sexual content involving a person under 18, grooming, or asking a minor for sexual content.

We comply with applicable child-safety laws, and we report illegal CSAM to the appropriate authority, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children where that applies, and to regional law enforcement.

How to report

In the app, use Report on a profile, in a chat, or during a call. You can also email hello@larkisedosti.app with the subject “Child safety”. Include the profile ID if you have it, and what you saw. Do not send illegal imagery in the email.

What we do

We review reports. Confirmed CSAE leads to removal of the content and the account, and a report to the relevant authority. Users can also Block someone so that person no longer appears in Discover, Match, or chats.

Uploaded photos stay in pending review and are not shown to other people until review is complete.
