Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete the Larki Se Dosti account from inside the app. That removes the account stored for you, including your name, username, age, and profile ID, and it deletes photos saved in the app on that phone.

In the app

  1. Open Larki Se Dosti.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Account & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email admin@dostiapp.online with the subject “Delete my account” and the profile ID shown on My Profile. There is no password. We will delete the account tied to that ID. Photos were stored only on the phone, so uninstalling the app also removes them from that phone.
