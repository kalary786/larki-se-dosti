Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete the Larki Se Dosti profile from inside the app. That removes the name, age, photos, chats, friends, and blocks stored on that device, and signs you out.

In the app

  1. Open Larki Se Dosti.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Account & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email hello@larkisedosti.app with the subject “Delete my account” and the profile ID shown on My Profile. There is no separate password account on a server. The profile lives on the device, and deletion there clears it.
