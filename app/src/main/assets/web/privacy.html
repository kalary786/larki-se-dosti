Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. Larki Se Dosti is only for people who are 18 or older.

What the app stores

Your profile is created on this device. That includes your name, age, gender, country, photos you choose, chats, friends, blocks, and coin balance. There is no password.

Photos

A photo you upload is marked pending review. Other people cannot see it until that review is finished. Until then, they see your name.

Safety tools

Report and Block are available on a profile, in a chat, and on a call. Blocking removes that person from your Discover, Match, and chats on this device.

What we do not do

We do not sell your profile. The app is not directed at children. If you are under 18, do not create an account.

Deletion

You can delete the account in the app under Account & Privacy. Steps and an email option are on the account deletion page.

Contact

Privacy questions: hello@larkisedosti.app.
