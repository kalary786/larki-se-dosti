Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Larki Se Dosti is a social app for adults aged 18 and older.

This policy explains what the app collects, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. It is written to match the app as it works today, and to meet Google Play’s requirement for a public privacy policy.

Who we are

Larki Se Dosti (“we”, “the app”) is operated at the contact address below. Privacy questions and deletion requests go to admin@dostiapp.online.

Who can use the app

The app is only for people who are 18 or older. You confirm your date of birth before an account is created. We do not aim the app at children, and we do not knowingly keep an account for anyone under 18.

Information you give us

When you create an account, we store:

  • a user ID created by Firebase Authentication (there is no password)
  • a public profile ID number
  • your username and display name
  • your date of birth, which we use to check that you are 18 or older and to show your age
  • your gender and country
  • whether a Google account is linked, if you choose that

This account information is uploaded to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so the profile can be opened again and so other signed-in adults can see the profile fields the app shows.

Photos

If you add a photo, it is saved only in this app’s private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to Firebase, and it is not shown to other people. Other people see your name, not your photo.

The photo picker lets you choose one picture. It does not give the app your whole gallery. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Photos are removed from the phone when you delete that photo, delete the account, or uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for this phone, app data may be included in that backup.

What other people can see

Other signed-in adults can see your display name, username, age, gender, country, and whether you appear online. The app shows your age, not your full date of birth. They cannot see your photos. Your account record still contains the date of birth, because that is how the app checks the age rule.

What stays on this phone

In this version, typed chats, friend lists, and reports stay on the phone. A block is saved to your account so it can be enforced. Your coin balance and coin history are saved with your account in Firebase, and a gift you send is saved in that chat. Closing the app does not reset the coin balance. Photos stay on the phone and are not uploaded.

Google sign-in

You can use the app without Google. If you link or sign in with Google, Google shares an account identifier with us so the same profile can be opened again. We never receive your Google password. Google’s own policy applies to that sign-in: policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use information

We use account information to create and show your profile, keep you signed in, apply the 18+ rule, show who is online, and delete the account when you ask. We do not sell your information. We do not use your photos for advertising. The app does not ask for your precise location.

Who processes it

Account information is processed by Google Firebase on our behalf. We do not give your profile to advertisers or data brokers. We may share information if the law requires it, or to report child sexual abuse material to the proper authority, as described on the child safety page.

How long we keep it

Account information, the coin balance, and coin history stay in Firebase until you delete the account, or until we delete it because it breaks the age rule or the terms. Photos stay on the phone until you delete them, delete the account, or uninstall the app. Typed chats kept only on the phone are not a server archive. Gift messages are saved with the account.

Deletion

In the app, open Profile, then Account & Privacy, then Delete account. That deletes the Firebase account, the username, and the photos stored on that phone. If you cannot open the app, email admin@dostiapp.online with the subject “Delete my account” and the profile ID from My Profile. Steps are also on the account deletion page.

Security

Only the signed-in owner can change that account. No app can promise perfect security. Use a phone only you control, and link Google if you want a way back to the same profile.

Children

Do not use the app if you are under 18. If we learn an account belongs to a child, we will delete it. Child-safety rules are on the child safety page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. The new policy applies from that date. For a change that affects how your account is stored, the date on this page is the notice.

Contact

Email admin@dostiapp.online. The terms for using the app are on the terms and conditions page.
